# Self-review (Phase 5)

Written as a sceptical reviewer at a top ML venue. Scores are 1–10. Each iteration lists the weaknesses
found, what was done about them, and what could not be fixed. The GPU budget (2 h) was exhausted during
iteration 1, so later fixes are CPU-only or analysis-only.

## Iteration 1 (after all CIFAR-10 runs, first paper draft)

| Criterion | Score | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Soundness | 6 | Equal-compute design, unbiased estimators, variance measured at identical parameters, pre-registered plan. But: tiny models, 3,000 steps, FIDs ≈ 140; n = 3; reduced FID schedule; probe sampling noise not quantified; the "oracle IS" result is only a prediction; the decomposition misses the measured antithetic ratio by ~0.2 at CIFAR step 3000. |
| Clarity | 7 | Clear question, consistent notation, every number generated by script. Figures are dense; the IS story ("helps early, hurts late") was stated more generally than the data support (it does not hold on Fashion-MNIST). |
| Significance | 5 | Mainly a careful replication plus a negative result. The antithetic analysis (the V_t + V_x < −ρV_ε condition) and the loss-vs-gradient proposal mismatch are useful, but small. |
| Reproducibility | 8 | Code embedded in kernels with file hashes; runs.csv; budget ledger; one-command regeneration of figures, tables and PDF. CIFAR checkpoints were not exported (size), so CIFAR probes cannot be re-run without GPU. |

### Ranked weaknesses
1. **Low statistical power / small scale.** n = 3, FID at one checkpoint for seeds 1–2, strat_anti FID n = 2.
   *Fix (analysis):* added a minimum-detectable-effect calculation (≈ 3.1 sd ⇒ ≈ 1.7 % held-out loss,
   ≈ 12 FID). The text now says "no large effect", not "no effect". *Cannot fix without more GPU.*
2. **Probe noise unquantified.** Variance ratios come from R = 48 (CIFAR) or 24 (Fashion-MNIST) draws, with
   no within-probe CI. *Fix (CPU):* re-probe saved Fashion-MNIST checkpoints with 4 independent probe seeds
   and report the spread (`scripts/probe_checkpoint.py`, `results/posthoc/`).
3. **The oracle gradient-IS claim is only a prediction.** *Fix (CPU):* at the same checkpoints, actually
   probe an IS estimator with proposal ∝ sqrt(E‖g‖² | t), estimated by an 8-repetition decomposition.
4. **Decomposition vs measurement mismatch** (antithetic on CIFAR: 1.15 predicted vs 0.92 measured;
   paired-i.i.d. on Fashion-MNIST: 1.70 vs 2.35). *Fix (CPU):* higher-repetition decomposition on
   Fashion-MNIST checkpoints, to see whether the gap is estimation noise. For CIFAR: report honestly.
5. **Over-general IS narrative.** *Fixed:* text now says the late-training reversal appears on CIFAR-10 only.
6. **Mechanism of the IS training gain is untested** (two conjectures in the paper). *Partially addressed:*
   ruled out gradient clipping (inactive after warm-up). Other mechanisms are labelled conjecture.
7. **Data provenance.** CIFAR-10 came from a Hugging Face mirror, not the authors' tarball. The logged
   pixel mean (120.708 over the train set) matches the standard CIFAR-10 channel means
   ((0.4914 + 0.4822 + 0.4465)/3 · 255 = 120.70), with 50,000/10,000 images. Noted in the paper.
8. **Novelty of antithetic training noise** rests on a web search (the arXiv API was unreachable). Hedged
   accordingly in the text.
